ABOUT OUR UNIVERSITY

A University with a Marine Focus

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Its history began in 1979 as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia.

In June 1996, the institution became Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). It was later known as Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) and then Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was upgraded to a full university and adopted its present name, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.
